## What this repository contains — and what it doesn't

- **No game content**: no code, art, audio, text, video, shaders or other files from either game, and nothing
  generated from them. The game is converted and compiled on the player's own computer, from the player's own copy,
  and that result never leaves it.
- **No keys, no firmware, no decryption or dumping tools.** Country-Box does not extract, decrypt or unpack anything.
- The repository holds Country-Box's own code, the recompilation core it builds on (with its licenses), and small
  lists of facts measured on the supported versions — file sizes and SHA-256 hashes used to check a copy, and shader
  hashes plus small hash-verified control fields used to prepare shaders. No screenshots or video of the games are included or
  shown. The launcher's art is drawn in code; fonts are open (SIL OFL) and downloaded from
  their official source at build time.

## Your copy

- To use Country-Box you need **your own copy of the game, obtained legally**, already extracted into `exefs/` and
  `romfs/` folders. Country-Box checks that the files match a supported version; it does not, and cannot, check where
  they came from.
- This project does not describe how to obtain a game and does not link to any source for one. Requests for that
  will be closed.
